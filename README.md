# PowerBI-Sales-Dashboard

This dashboard is designed to help understand how a business is performing. It shows total sales, profit, and the number of products sold. You can also see which products, categories, states, or customers are doing best, and how sales change over time. The goal is to make it easy for anyone to see trends, find opportunities, and make better business decisions.


## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales (Sum of Amount):** Total revenue generated.  
- **Total Profit:** Overall profit from sales.  
- **Quantity Sold:** Number of products sold.  
- **Average Selling Price:** Amount divided by quantity.  
- **Quarterly Performance:** Sales and profit trends over time.

---

## Insights
- Q4 has the **highest sales**, indicating seasonal trends.  
- Certain **states are performing better**, which can help in focusing marketing efforts.  
- **Some product categories and sub-categories are top performers**, bringing the most revenue.  
- A few **top customers contribute significantly** to total sales.  
- **Payment methods show customer preferences**, useful for promotions and strategy.  
- Sales and profits vary across quarters, helping plan for peak seasons.


---

## Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop** – for creating the dashboard  
- **Excel / CSV** – dataset for analysis

---

## How to Use
1. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.  
2. Explore the dashboard using filters for **state, category, quarter, or customer**.  
3. Hover over visuals to see exact numbers and trends.



- All data is **fictional/sample data** for demonstration purposes.  
- Insights are based on aggregated totals and trends from the dataset.
